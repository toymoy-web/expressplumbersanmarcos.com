Shower & Bathtub Installation in San Marcos, CA

Shower and bathtub installation covers the plumbing side of a bathroom remodel or fixture replacement, supply lines, drain connections, and valve placement, the work that has to be right before any tile or finish surface goes in, since correcting a plumbing mistake after the fact means tearing back out what was just installed.

How We Diagnose the Problem

For a straight fixture swap, confirming that existing supply and drain locations match the new unit's requirements is the first step, a mismatch in rough-in dimensions is a common surprise on older bathrooms being updated with modern fixtures. For a full remodel, the plumbing plan is coordinated with the broader project scope, including where valves and showerheads will actually sit relative to the finished layout.

Signs You Need This Service

  • A shower valve that's aging, difficult to adjust, or leaking behind the wall
  • A bathtub that's cracked, chipped, or simply due for replacement
  • Planning a bathroom remodel that includes a new shower or tub
  • Converting a tub to a walk-in shower or vice versa
  • Water damage discovered behind an old shower or tub surround during renovation

Shower & Bathtub Installation Pricing & What Affects It

A like-for-like tub or shower valve replacement is more straightforward and less costly than a layout change or full remodel coordination, which involves more planning and often waiting on other trades. Pricing reflects the actual scope once rough-in compatibility is confirmed.

Rough-In Compatibility on Older Homes

Older San Marcos homes were often plumbed to different standard dimensions than modern fixtures assume, which means a straightforward-sounding tub or shower swap can occasionally require adjusting supply line or drain positioning to match the new unit, something confirmed before ordering fixtures, not discovered mid-installation.

Tub-to-Shower Conversions

Converting a bathtub to a walk-in shower involves more than just removing the tub, it typically means relocating the drain to the shower's designated location and adjusting the valve height and position for standing use rather than bathing, plus waterproofing considerations specific to a shower pan versus a tub base.

Coordinating With Remodel Timelines

On a full remodel, plumbing work generally happens before tile, drywall finishing, and fixtures are installed, since the rough plumbing needs to be in its final position and pressure-tested before it's sealed behind a finished wall. Getting this sequencing right avoids the far more costly scenario of opening finished work to fix a plumbing issue.

Valve Types for New Installations

A pressure-balancing or thermostatic shower valve maintains consistent water temperature even if another fixture in the house draws water simultaneously, a meaningful upgrade over older single-handle valves that can produce a sudden temperature swing when, for example, a toilet is flushed elsewhere in the home.

Waterproofing Behind Tile Showers

A tiled shower relies on a waterproof membrane behind the tile, not the tile and grout themselves, to actually keep water out of the wall structure, this is installed as part of the plumbing and substrate work before tile goes up, and getting it right is what actually prevents the kind of hidden water damage that surfaces years later as a mystery leak.

Accessible and Curbless Shower Installations

A curbless or low-threshold shower, often requested for accessibility or a cleaner modern look, requires more careful drain slope and waterproofing planning than a standard shower with a curb, since there's no raised barrier helping contain water, the drain placement and floor pitch need to be right the first time, since correcting it later means removing finished flooring.

Freestanding Tub Plumbing Considerations

A freestanding tub, popular in many bathroom remodels, needs its plumbing supply either routed up through the floor at a precise location or via a floor-mounted filler, both of which require exact measurements matched to the specific tub model before rough plumbing goes in, a detail that's much harder to correct after flooring is finished than to plan correctly up front.

Bathroom Remodels in Older and Newer Homes

Bathroom remodels drive most shower and tub work. Older homes near Lake San Marcos often need rough-in changes to fit modern valves, while new townhomes use standard dimensions.

Coordination with your tile contractor matters in both cases. We set valves and drains before the walls close.

Shower Valve and Showerhead Leaks

A showerhead that drips after turning off usually has a worn cartridge or valve. A leak inside the wall is more serious, because water can travel out of sight.

If a wall leak is suspected, the leak detection page covers how it is traced.

Tub Faucet and Spout Problems

A tub spout that drips or sprays from the diverter, or a handle that leaks, usually needs a part replaced. Tell us the style of fixture to help match parts.

A Slow or Standing Drain in the Tub or Shower

Hair and soap are the usual culprits. If the drain stays slow after cleaning, the line deeper down may be restricted, and drain cleaning can address it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you install a new shower valve without redoing the whole wall?+

Often yes for a like-for-like valve replacement at the same location, a full layout change requires more wall access.

How long does converting a tub to a shower take?+

The plumbing portion is usually completed in a day, though the full conversion including tile and waterproofing takes longer and involves other trades.

Do you coordinate with contractors on a full bathroom remodel?+

Yes, plumbing rough-in and final connections are typically coordinated closely with the overall remodel timeline and every single other trade involved throughout the whole project.

My shower valve leaks inside the wall. Can you repair it?+

Valve repair or replacement is within the service. The wall may need a small opening to reach the valve, and the plumber explains the options before cutting.

Can you replace a leaking tub faucet or spout?+

Yes. Tell the dispatcher the brand or style if you know it, and the plumber can plan the right parts.

How long does a shower or tub installation take?+

The timeline depends on the scope, access, and whether other trades are involved. Ask for an estimate when you describe the project.
