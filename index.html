Plumbing in San Marcos's Housing Stock

San Marcos sits in North County inland San Diego County, with a housing mix that ranges from older homes on original copper or galvanized lines to newer construction built on PEX. San Diego County's naturally hard water is a well-documented factor in shortened water heater lifespan. Scale buildup inside the tank and on heating elements is one of the most common reasons a water heater fails years earlier than it should. That same hard water is also why whole-home filtration systems and recirculating hot-water pumps have become a common upgrade request from homeowners in this area, rather than a one-off niche request.

Any plumbing work in California falls under state contractor licensing rules (C-36 for plumbing specifically). Ask about license and insurance details when you call, and expect a straight answer.
